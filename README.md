# Ecommerce-Insights-and-Optimization
E-commerce data analysis using Python, SQL, Excel and Power BI

## 📌 Project Overview

This project focuses on analyzing e-commerce data to understand product prices, product categories, and shipping costs. Python, SQL, Excel, and Power BI are used to prepare, analyze, and visualize the data to generate useful business insights.

## 🎯 Project Objectives

* Analyze product prices across different categories.
* Identify categories with high total shipping costs.
* Compare average product prices across categories.
* Identify top products based on aggregated prices.
* Create a dashboard to present the analysis and business insights.

## 🛠️ Tools and Technologies

* **Python:** Data cleaning and preparation using Pandas.
* **SQL:** Data queries, joins, and analysis.
* **Microsoft Excel:** Data analysis and PivotTables.
* **Power BI:** Dashboard creation and data visualization.

## 📂 Project Files

The repository contains the following project deliverables:

* **Python Notebook:** Data cleaning and preparation.
* **SQL Script:** SQL queries used for analysis.
* **Excel Workbook:** PivotTables and summarized analysis.
* **Power BI Dashboard:** Visual representation of key metrics and findings.
* **Final Report:** Project overview, business insights, recommendations, and limitations.
* **Data Dictionary:** Description of dataset columns and their meanings.
* **Presentation:** Summary of the project, if included.

## 🔍 Analysis Performed

The project examines:

1. Total price by product category.
2. Total shipping cost by product category.
3. Average price by product category.
4. Top 10 products based on aggregated price.
5. Relationship between product price and shipping cost.

## 💡 Key Business Insights

* The `relogios_presentes` category has a high total aggregated price in the analysis.
* The `cama_mesa_banho` category has a high total shipping cost.
* The `beleza_saude` category is among the leading categories by total aggregated price.
* Average product prices vary across categories.
* Product-level price aggregation helps identify products with high total prices.

## 📈 Business Recommendations

* Review category performance to identify business opportunities.
* Analyze shipping costs and explore ways to reduce logistics expenses.
* Promote categories that show strong aggregated price performance.
* Review pricing differences across product categories.
* Monitor product-level performance regularly to support business decisions.

## ⚠️ Limitations

* The available data does not provide all the information required to calculate actual profit.
* Some product details may be missing from the original data.
* Total shipping cost alone does not show the relative shipping expense for each order.
* Price-based analysis does not account for all expenses, discounts, or returns.

## ✅ Conclusion

This project demonstrates how Python, SQL, Excel, and Power BI can be used together to analyze e-commerce data. The analysis provides insights into product prices, category performance, and shipping costs to support data-driven business decisions.
